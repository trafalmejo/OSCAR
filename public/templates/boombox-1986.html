Side A · Inlay card

What every control sends

Each control sends OSC to the address below. Select one in the editor to aim it somewhere else.

POWER
/boombox/power
1 on, 0 off.
Tape keys
/boombox/rec /boombox/play /boombox/pause
They latch, as the real keys did: 1 down, 0 up. The reels turn while PLAY is down.
REW, F.FWD, STOP
/boombox/rew /boombox/ff /boombox/stop
1 while held.
TUNING
/boombox/tuning
88 to 108, in steps of 0.1.
FUNCTION
/boombox/function
1 tape, 2 radio, 3 line in, as a whole number.
VOLUME, BALANCE
/boombox/volume /boombox/balance
0 to 1, and -1 to 1.
Graphic equaliser
/boombox/eq/100 ... /boombox/eq/10k
-12 to 12, a decibel at a time.
Level lights
/boombox/level/left /boombox/level/right
These listen. Send them 0 to 1 and they light.

Delete this card from the layout when the unit goes out on the street.
