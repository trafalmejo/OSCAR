Channel map

9-channel mode

Set the fixture to 9-channel mode at DMX address 1. Everything here sends DMX on universe 1, Art-Net, broadcast; nothing sends OSC.

Every control
DMX out on, OSC out off
Universe 1, Art-Net, broadcast. To drive a USB interface instead: select every widget, set Protocol to USB. To name a node, set Node or port.
CH1, CH2
Pan, tilt
The pad drives both at once; the faders drive each alone. Highest takes precedence where they overlap.
CH3
Off, dimmer, strobe, switch
The fader is the dimmer, 0 to 134, full at the top. Strobe and switch are past that: the dropdown picks them by their exact value.
CH4 to CH6
Red, green, blue
The colour picker sends all three from one swatch. The programs light one at full.
CH7
White
A fader, and a program at full.
CH8
Pan and tilt speed
The FAST MOVES program sends full.
CH9
Reset
The fixture resets between 150 and 200; the dropdown sends 175, then back to 0.
FLASH
Full while held
Each strip's key sends 255 on its channel for as long as it is down, over the fader. Not on CH3 or CH9, where 255 is not a level.
Programs
DMX, and the faders follow
A program lights its channels and also tells OSCAR itself, over OSC to 127.0.0.1 port 9000, so the faders on those channels move with it and drop when it is switched off. If OSCAR listens on another port (the top bar says), select the six programs and set Port.

A second fixture at address 10 in the same mode: copy the desk and add 9 to every Channel.
