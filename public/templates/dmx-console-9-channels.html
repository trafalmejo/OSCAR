Channel map

9-channel mode

Set the fixture to 9-channel mode at DMX address 1. Everything here sends DMX to an Open DMX USB interface, the first found; nothing sends OSC.

The faders
The only controls on the wire
USB: Open DMX, on the first FTDI interface found; leave Node or port blank, or name the port. For an Enttec Pro or DMXKing, or a network node: select every widget and set Protocol.
The rest
Work the faders, and follow them
The pad, the swatch, the dropdowns and the programs send nothing of their own: each moves its faders, and the faders send. Move a fader and the pad, the swatch and the dropdowns follow. So no two controls ever disagree about a channel.
CH1, CH2
Pan, tilt
The pad is faders 1 and 2, and they are the pad.
CH3
Off, dimmer, strobe, switch
0 to 7 off, 8 to 134 dimmer, 135 to 239 strobe, 240 up switch. The dropdown puts fader 3 at a mode's exact value.
CH4 to CH6
Red, green, blue
The swatch is faders 4, 5 and 6, and they are the swatch. The programs light one at full.
CH7
White
A fader, and a program at full.
CH8
Pan and tilt speed
0 is fastest, 255 slowest on this fixture; SLOW MOVES puts fader 8 at full.
CH9
Reset
The fixture resets between 150 and 200; the dropdown puts fader 9 at 175, then back to 0.
FLASH
Full while held
Each strip's key sends 255 on its channel for as long as it is down, over the fader. Not on CH3 or CH9, where 255 is not a level.
Programs
They work the faders
A program sends nothing of its own: on, it puts the faders of its channels at full, and they send; off, it puts them at 0. The script at the end of the page says which faders each one works.

A second fixture at address 10 in the same mode: copy the desk and add 9 to every Channel.
