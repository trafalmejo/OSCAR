Getting started

A thousand cues in your pocket

Each control sends OSC to the address below. Select one in the editor to aim it somewhere else.

The wheel
/player/wheel
Two numbers, x then y, each from -1 to 1: where the thumb is on the wheel. The angle is atan2(y, x); turn that into volume, or a scroll, at the other end.
MENU, PREV, NEXT, centre
/player/menu /player/prev /player/next /player/select
1 while held.
PLAY / PAUSE
/player/play
It latches: 1 playing, 0 paused. The display shows which.
Scrub bar
/player/position
0 to 1: how far through the song.
Playlist
/player/playlist
1 to 4, as a whole number.
HOLD
/player/hold
0 or 1, as a whole number.
Battery
/player/battery
This listens. Send 0 to 1 and the battery on the display fills.

Delete this card from the layout when the player goes in a pocket.
