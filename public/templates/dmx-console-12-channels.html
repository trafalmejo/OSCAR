How it works

12 channels, one effect

Channels 1 to 12, on an Open DMX USB interface, the first found; nothing sends OSC. Patch your fixtures at addresses in that range and write their names on the scribble strips.

The faders
Channels 1 to 12, one each
Only the faders and the FLASH keys send DMX. Everything else in the effect section drives the faders, and the faders send.
SEL
Which channels the effect moves
Light the SEL key of each channel the effect should move. Start an effect with nothing selected and all twelve are selected for you.
Effects
Sine, triangle, square, saw, random, chase
Press one to start it on the selected channels; press it again, or STOP, to stop. The faders stay where they were when it stopped. Chase lights one channel at a time, in order; random picks a new level every beat.
Speed
Beats per minute, 20 to 300
One cycle of the wave, or one chase step, per beat. TAP sets it from the gap between your last taps.
Size, base
How far, and from where
The fader moves between Base and Base plus Size, clipped at 255. Size 255 and Base 0 is the full travel.
Spread
0 to 100% of a cycle
At 0 every selected channel moves together. Raise it and each one runs a little behind the one before, so the effect travels along the desk as a wave; at 100% they are spaced over one whole cycle. Chase and random ignore it.
A hand wins
Touch a fader to take it back
Moving a fader that the effect is moving, here or on another device, or a level arriving for it from outside, takes that channel out of the effect: its SEL goes off and the fader is yours.
FLASH
Full while held
Each strip's key sends 255 on its channel for as long as it is down, over the fader.
BLACKOUT
Every fader to 0
Stops the effect and puts all twelve faders at 0.

Run an effect from one device at a time. Another device sees the faders move and the effect key lit, and can stop it, take a channel back or black out.
