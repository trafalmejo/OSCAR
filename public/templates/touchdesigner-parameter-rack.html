Quick reference

Two CHOPs

An OSC In CHOP hears everything here as channels named after the addresses. An OSC Out CHOP, pointed at OSCAR's IP and listening port, sends channels back, and the meters follow them.

OSC In CHOP
Network Port: the port every widget here sends to
One channel per address: td/speed, td/scale, td/position/x ... Wire them to your parameters with a Select or an Export.
Faders
/td/speed /td/scale /td/hue /td/feedback
Floats. Speed runs 0 to 2, the rest 0 to 1.
Position
/td/position
Two floats, x then y, -1 to 1. Two channels in the CHOP.
Colour
/td/colour
Three floats, r g b, 0 to 1.
Scenes
/td/scene/1 .. /td/scene/3
1 while held, 0 let go: an edge to trigger on.
Caption
/td/caption
A string. An OSC In DAT reads it; a CHOP does not carry text.
OSC Out CHOP
Network Address: OSCAR's IP. Port: its listening port
Name the channels td/audio/level and td/sensor/distance, 0 to 1, and the two meters follow them.

Tick "Data in" on any fader and it follows the channel of the same name coming back, so a parameter animated inside TouchDesigner shows here too.
