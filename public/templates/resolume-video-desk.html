Quick reference

Resolume over OSC

Preferences > OSC in Resolume: Input on, port 7000. Set every widget here to that computer and port. Output on, at OSCAR's IP and listening port, and the faders follow Resolume.

Clips
/composition/layers/L/clips/C/connect
1 while the screen is held. L is the layer, C the column. Add rows and columns by copying a strip.
Columns
/composition/columns/C/connect
1 while held: every clip in that column starts.
Opacity
/composition/layers/L/video/opacity
0 to 1. Data in is on, so the fader follows what Resolume shows.
CLR / BYP / SOLO
/composition/layers/L/clear /bypassed /solo
CLR sends 1 while held. BYP and SOLO are toggles: 1 on, 0 off.
Crossfader
/composition/crossfader/phase
0 is A, 1 is B. Follows Resolume.
Master
/composition/master
0 to 1. Follows Resolume.
Tempo
/composition/tempocontroller/tempo
BPM, 60 to 200, sent whole.
TAP / RESYNC
/composition/tempocontroller/tempotap /resync
1 while held.
PANIC
/composition/disconnectall
1 while held: every clip stops.

Resolume echoes a clip's state as well, but a clip is a key, not a fader: what it shows is your finger's. Layer 3 is on top, as in Resolume.
