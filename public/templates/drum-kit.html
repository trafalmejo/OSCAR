Drum Kit

MIDI channel 10 · General MIDI drums
OSCAR

Every pad is a MIDI note

Channel 10, the drum channel of General MIDI, to the first MIDI output this computer has; on Windows the built-in synth plays it. Select a pad in the editor to send it to another port, another note or channel, or to switch OSC on as well. The keyboard plays the kit too.
